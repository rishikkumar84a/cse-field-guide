# Community conduct

CSE Field Guide welcomes learners and contributors with different backgrounds
and levels of experience. Critique claims and code with evidence. Give specific,
constructive feedback and respect a contributor's time and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, deliberate disclosure of
private information, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

The maintainer may request revisions, remove harmful contributions, or restrict
participation when needed to keep project spaces useful and respectful. Reports
should describe the behavior and relevant context without republishing private
information. Contact the maintainer through https://rishik.tech to arrange a
private reporting channel. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior
and its impact.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces maintained for this project.
